# Disclaimer

Safety Net is a portfolio prototype built and tested exclusively with fictional synthetic
transcripts, author-defined synthetic labels, and author-defined synthetic registry records.

It has not processed real patient data, recordings, production systems, independently validated
ground truth, or completed independent domain validation. It demonstrates workflow mechanics, not
model accuracy or clinical, medical-device, pharmacovigilance, regulatory, or production
performance.

It does not provide medical advice; determine adverse events, seriousness, causality,
reportability, or day zero; perform validated MedDRA coding; submit reports; replace qualified
reviewers, approved SOPs, validated case systems, reporting obligations, or legal requirements; or
establish fitness for any operational purpose.

Displayed deadlines are simulated internal workflow controls, not regulatory clocks. Displayed
metrics describe functional behaviour on small, author-created synthetic datasets and must not be
interpreted as real-world sensitivity, specificity, accuracy, compliance, or validation.

The video and screenshots are unchanged historical v1.0 artifacts. They predate the Guided Journey
and held-out evidence, and their terminology should be read together with the corrections and
provenance notice in the current [README](README.md) and [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).
