# Disclaimer

Safety Net is a portfolio prototype built and tested exclusively with fictional, synthetic data.

It is not a medical device, clinical decision-support system, validated pharmacovigilance system,
or production service. It does not provide medical advice, determine whether an adverse event
exists, decide reportability, perform validated medical coding, replace qualified professionals, or
replace an organisation's approved procedures and legal obligations.

Displayed metrics describe functional behaviour on small, author-created synthetic datasets. They
must not be interpreted as real-world sensitivity, specificity, accuracy, regulatory compliance, or
fitness for any operational purpose.
